# Promotion suggestions

Read-only local association model over net fulfilled customer baskets. Preserve
tenant authority, POS/linked-sales deduplication and returns from existing sales
evidence. Transfers affect stock reserves but never customer basket affinity.
Require joint basket support and lift >1 before recommending a learned combo.
No external model calls, automatic price changes or estimated causal uplift.

Discounts are preview-only and constrained by current known pre-tax retail/cost,
currency, a configurable ceiling/margin floor and active promotion conflicts.
Cost/margin facts are owner/admin only; staff suggestions cannot infer protected
cost through automatic discount calculations. Unknown data requires review.
Stock guard protects current minimum and pending commitments at each location.
Never combine locations or currencies into a bundle. Local fixed celebrations
are calendar occasions, not claims of officially observed public holidays;
movable/custom events use entered dates. Dates use Africa/Lagos.

Whole-unit spare stock must support at least one offer. Suggestions rank by
opportunity score with stable key ties before the bounded 500-result response.
Promotion conflicts match the actual central catalogue category/brand and exact
size restrictions, with exclusions honored. Unknown taxonomy stays a manual
stacking review; unrelated categories or brands do not block valid previews.

Global drinks calendar sources/rules live in drink-events.md. Curated dates feed
60-day occasions and central Product type/subType matching; exact selected
catalogue name/date retains the verified identity. Published single-year events
are omitted in other years until verified. Calendar timing does not predict lift.
