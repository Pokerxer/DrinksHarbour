## Funding strategy for the USD250,000 objective

[Likely — recommendation, not funding availability] Keep USD250k as an overall, budget-supported objective, while making the first funded milestone smaller: prove that Abuja beverage businesses repeatedly use and pay for the ERM software. Small grants and commercial pilots can finance validation; larger funding becomes more credible with collected receipts and retention.

1. **Now:** prioritise SMEDAN; check Wema terms and application availability; make a targeted Jobtech expression of interest if live-user evidence exists. Do not assume awards can be stacked or charge the same costs to two funders.
2. **Next90days:** convert pilots into collected subscription revenue, document repeat usage, support cost and tenant outcomes. Example internal planning target:5–10paying tenants with three months of usage/collections. This is a proposed milestone, not an organiser requirement or projection.
3. **Then:** revisit matched grants and corporate pilots. If the wholeUSD250k is urgently needed, broaden to negotiated angel/equity capital or commercial sponsorship. Grants alone are not a verified financing plan.
4. **Before applying:** produce a bottom-up12–18month budget, with a smaller pilot budget that can stand alone. Separate engineering, onboarding, compliance, sales, infrastructure and contingency. No salary/runway or return forecast can be validated without actual costs.

Do not convert foreign grant amounts to naira using an assumed exchange rate. Do not add grants, repayable loans, cloud credits, advisory services, pooled prize totals or contingent follow-on investments into a single “available funding” total.

## Positioning and evidence

[Likely] Lead with the real B2B value: “DrinksHarbour helps Nigerian beverage businesses manage stock, sales and online orders through a shared software platform.” Disclose the alcohol-facing marketplace and planned revenue streams. Do not hide the sector behind a genericSME label or imply proven employment/waste outcomes.

No blanket alcohol exclusion applicable to every shortlisted programme was established in the reviewed official material. Absence of a visible prohibition is not approval. Confirm whether software serving alcohol sellers, marketplace commissions and marketing expenditure are allowed before lengthy applications or accepting funding.

Prepare once and reuse:

- CAC evidence and exact registered entity name/type; SMEDAN ID where needed; tax and corporate bank details requested by the funder.
- Founder profile, team roles, product demo, live URL and an8–10slide deck.
- Accurate current traction: zero collected revenue; distinguish demos, registered users, pilot businesses, signedLOIs and paying customers.
- Customer interview/pilot evidence, permissions for testimonials, and a separate milestone budget for each award size.
- Reporting and disbursement terms; restricted costs, matching funds, loan/equity/IP obligations, travel and attendance requirements.
