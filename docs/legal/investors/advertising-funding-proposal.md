# DrinksHarbour Advertising Funding Proposal

PROPOSED TERMS FOR DISCUSSION

Prepared on 7 October 2026. The founder wants to fund advertising for both paying beverage businesses and online shoppers without giving a lender approximately 14% equity. Recommend a staged non-convertible company loan with no shares, warrants or conversion rights. This is a funding proposal, not a signed facility or lender commitment.

## Proposed facility

| Term | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Maximum facility | NGN 5,000,000 |
| Draws | NGN 1m pilot, then NGN 2m and NGN 2m against agreed results and cash-flow gates |
| Interest | Opening offer of 8% annually, simple and non-compounding |
| Term | 36 months for each draw from its own funding date |
| Grace | No scheduled payments in the first six months; interest accrues separately |
| Repayment | Thirty monthly principal payments plus grace-period interest and declining-balance interest |
| Ownership rights | None; no equity conversion or perpetual profit participation |
| Early repayment | Permitted without penalty; unpaid principal and accrued interest remain due |
| Security | Unsecured proposed; no founder guarantee or security over code/domain |
| Undrawn funds | No interest or commitment fee proposed |

Eight percent is a negotiating offer, not a current Nigerian market quotation or a rate the lender must accept. The lender faces early-stage credit risk and may request different terms or decline. Debt obligations remain even if advertising fails. Further draw commitments and objective conditions must be expressly agreed, not assumed from this proposal.

## Evidence before the next NGN 2m

For the business channel, seek at least four additional campaign-attributed active tenants, each with two recurring paid collections. At the complete NGN 600k budget, four tenants imply NGN 150k acquisition cost each. Include creative, sales, onboarding and appropriately allocated shared expenses, not just media spend. Proposed payback threshold is at most six months using actual contribution after provider fees, discounts and variable support.

The prior illustrative tenant mix gives NGN 45k average monthly subscription less NGN 775 provider fees and NGN 5k variable service cost: NGN 39,225 contribution before fixed overhead. NGN 150k acquisition cost would imply 3.82-month payback. Four such tenants would add NGN 180k gross recurring income and NGN 156,900 contribution before fixed costs. These are assumptions and targets, not campaign forecasts.

For shoppers, test fulfilled-order contribution separately. At an illustrative NGN 25k basket and 11% effective platform share, gross platform earnings are NGN 2,750. Estimated provider/payout, fulfilment support and loss-reserve costs of 6% of sales leave NGN 1,250 before acquisition. A proposed all-in acquisition target of at most NGN 625 per fulfilled order would leave NGN 625 contribution. Actual tenant agreements and costs can change this substantially.

At those assumptions, NGN 400k shopper spend needs 320 fulfilled orders to break even, or 640 at the NGN 625 acquisition target. Start with a NGN 30k media test inside the NGN 200k shopper media allocation; verify settled orders and full acquisition costs before scaling. Do not treat clicks, registrations, full marketplace GMV or predicted repeat purchases as Company cash contribution.

Later draws require positive settled channel economics, retained paid customers and an affordable downside cash forecast. Pause failing channels and reallocate only under the agreed budget process. The last NGN 2m requires equivalent evidence for the expanded cohort. No marketing copy, ad launch or purchase has occurred.

## Revenue-linked alternative

If fixed payments are unsuitable, negotiate a non-convertible revenue-linked structure. For illustration, 10% of defined collected Company receipts until total payments reach 1.2 times actual draws would target NGN 6m for a fully drawn NGN 5m facility. This is a capped finance charge, not 20% yearly interest or permanent revenue ownership. Tenant settlements, taxes, refunds and wallet/gift liabilities must be excluded; the contract must state how provider and fulfilment costs affect the payment base.

At a NGN 120k payment base, the payment would be only NGN 12k and NGN 6m would take 500 months without growth. At NGN 2m, NGN 200k monthly payments would take 30 payment months. With three months of grace and a 36-month long-stop, the average base over the remaining 33 months must be approximately NGN 1.818m. An unpaid maturity balance, extension or balloon must be expressly negotiated; no debt forgiveness is assumed. This alternative is not established as affordable or available.
