## Evidence and commercial reasoning

Reviewed code integrates tenant billing, shared catalogue/tenant stock, inventory, POS, purchasing, accounting, online order payout snapshots and venue workflows. A buyer may value useful development already completed and time saved rather than the two Nigerian tenants' revenue. Two paying tenants provide limited real-use evidence; they do not establish foreign product fit.

[Acquire.com's buyer guidance](https://help.acquire.com/buyer-faqs-1) explains that revenue alone can be an ineffective measure for a very small startup and useful assets matter. This does not appraise DrinksHarbour. Its [early-stage guidance](https://help.acquire.com/what-pre-revenue-businesses-are-allowed) focuses on functionality, transferability and confidence in taking over. No listing, buyer demand or marketplace eligibility is claimed.

For a build-versus-buy sensitivity only, [U.S. BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm) reports May2025 median annual pay of135,980 USD for developers and104,300 for QA analysts/testers. Assume8–12 developer person-months,2 QA person-months, and an estimated25% ancillary/employment/project overhead allowance. The resulting illustrative U.S.-staffed budget is approximately135,046–191,704 USD. Effort and overhead are analyst assumptions, not a measured DrinksHarbour rebuild, salary quotation, contractor rate or minimum cost. This assumes reusable templates/libraries and a useful subset of scope. AI-assisted rebuilding, offshore developers, standard SaaS or reduced requirements could cost far less.

An illustrative50–75% commercial/transferability discount gives approximately34k–96k of software utility in that sensitivity, broadly overlapping the exclusive proposal. The discount is judgment, not transaction data; the calculation informs but does not establish a selling price. Non-exclusive licences are priced below assignment because the seller can retain/reuse the IP and the buyer acquires narrower rights. No business revenue multiple or separate domain premium is added.

Important limits: earlier offline backend verification passed3405 tests with2 skipped; a turnkey foreign release, frontend build, live billing, mail/jobs and buyer environment were not all verified. The admin originated from an Isomorphic template. Third-party code, templates, fonts, images and catalogue content need a licence/redistribution review before promising a source-code sale. Transfer only original rights owned or assets lawfully sublicensable. Nigeria-specific currency, payment, tax, beverage and operating policies require a buyer scope review; foreign readiness is not assumed.

## Recommended offer

Offer the35k non-exclusive commercial source licence first if the objective is raising cash while retaining the Nigerian business. Define the buyer's allowed hosting, tenants, territories, modifications, branding, resale/sublicensing and exclusivity explicitly. A general right to use source does not silently authorise unrestricted resale.

Price assumes an agreed repository snapshot, accepted feature list and build/deployment documentation, subject to a rights review and clean release acceptance. Buyer-specific migration, hosting, gateway replacement, tax/localisation changes and ongoing support are separately scoped and priced. Do not promise unlimited customisation or perpetual free maintenance.

Agree acceptance tests, handover scope, support hours, payment/escrow milestones, ownership of adaptations, licence/IP warranties and liability. For an exclusive transaction, preserve any required right to operate for existing tenants expressly. Customer data, secrets and live production access are not included merely for evaluation. Counsel appropriate to the actual jurisdictions should draft/review the licence or assignment. No quote, code, customer records or external message has been sent.

To narrow the price: buyer country, internal use versus operating a commercial platform, licence versus exclusive ownership, required functionality, integrations/localisation, release evidence and actual offers. The earlier fundraising valuation and funding instruments remain separate; no contracts or ownership change by this proposal.
