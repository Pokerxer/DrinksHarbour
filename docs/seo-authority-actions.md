## Concrete next actions for the business owner

1. Export referring-domain URLs from SiteGuru or Search Console. Review actual
   sources before acting; the summary score alone does not justify disavowal.
2. Ask existing authorised suppliers/distributors whether they maintain a
   stockist directory. Supply the relevant DrinksHarbour brand page, verified
   business name and contact details; request inclusion only where the trading
   relationship is real. Start with brands already stocked, not invented partners.
3. Check the business's existing public profiles for the correct canonical
   website (`https://www.drinksharbour.com/`), address and phone. Claim or update
   listings through the owner's accounts, without duplicate or fictional locations.
4. Pitch existing buying guides that already earn search impressions to relevant
   Nigerian food, hospitality and drinks editors. Start with Macallan comparisons
   and Chardonnay guides. Offer useful research and named expertise, not a paid
   link disguised as editorial endorsement. Any sponsorship must be disclosed.
5. Run [SiteGuru competitor comparison](https://app.siteguru.co/backlink_results/opportunities/554078)
   for Drinks.ng and WineShopAbuja if the owner chooses to use the paid analysis.
   Review relevance and genuine relationships before pursuing any source.

## Measurement

Track new relevant referring domains, their linking pages, referral visits and
Search Console non-brand clicks. Record the next provider refresh alongside the
baseline. Do not promise a target rank or deadline: discovery and valuation are
controlled by third parties. No messages, purchases, submissions or public-profile
edits have been made in this session.
