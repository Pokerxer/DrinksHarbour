# Wyn City 360 Degree Staff Appraisal Question Bank

## Purpose

This question bank supports a 360-degree appraisal for Wyn City staff. It is designed around observable daily activities, service standards, teamwork, accountability, safety, and department-specific responsibilities. Each question can be answered by the employee, manager, or a relevant peer.

## Recommended response scale

- **1 — Rarely:** The behaviour is seldom demonstrated.
- **2 — Inconsistently:** The behaviour is demonstrated occasionally and needs improvement.
- **3 — Usually:** The expected standard is met in most situations.
- **4 — Consistently:** The expected standard is met reliably.
- **5 — Outstanding:** The behaviour is consistently demonstrated at an exceptional level.
- **N/O — Not observed:** The reviewer has not seen enough evidence to rate the behaviour.

Reviewers should rate only behaviours they have directly observed during the appraisal period and add a short example where a rating is 1, 2, or 5.
