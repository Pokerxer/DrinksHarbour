**Description**

<p>Reif Estate Winery is a family winery in Niagara-on-the-Lake, Ontario. Its vineyards lie in the Niagara River sub-appellation, where air movement along the river can help reduce the risk of spring and autumn frosts.</p>
<p>The estate traces its first vineyard plantings to 1977. Those vines form part of a site that the winery uses to grow grape varieties suited to the relatively warm conditions of this part of Niagara.</p>
<p>Browse the individual bottles to compare grape varieties, styles and sweetness. The estate name identifies the producer; the bottle label tells you which wine you are choosing.</p>
<p>For an icewine, explore <a href='/product/reif-estate-grand-reserve-riesling-icewine-2019'>Grand Reserve Riesling Icewine 2019</a>. For a different style, compare <a href='/product/reif-estate-winery-chardonnay'>Reif Estate Chardonnay</a> and <a href='/product/reif-estate-winery-cabernet-sauvignon-2021'>Reif Estate Cabernet Sauvignon 2021</a>.</p>

**Story**

The Reif family's winemaking roots reach back to Germany. Ewald Reif brought that family tradition to Ontario, where the estate began taking shape along the Niagara River.

The family established the winery in 1982. Klaus Reif later carried on the business, continuing the family's connection to the Niagara-on-the-Lake vineyard.

**Story**

The Château Vartely brand launched in 2004. Its name refers to Orhei and an older Hungarian name that the company translates as “place of the fortress.”

In 2008 the winery opened a visitor complex. The project added a place for guests to learn about the winery and taste its wines alongside its production business.
