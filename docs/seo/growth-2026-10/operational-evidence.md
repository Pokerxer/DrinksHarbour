# Claims and operational evidence

The public authenticity page is buying/checking/support guidance. It does not
invent a laboratory test, a brand partnership or a bottle-by-bottle inspection
audit. Existing site policy is not independent proof that all operational checks
actually happen. Obtain the evidence below before strengthening public claims.

| Claim or content | Current implementation | Evidence needed for stronger wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approved sellers | Uses platform approval terminology and existing seller directory | Current seller onboarding/approval criteria and accountable owner |

| Authenticity guarantee | Public page explains commitment, buyer checks and existing returns/support process | Defined guarantee scope, evidence handling and agreed remedy terms before promising unconditional refunds |
| FCT same-day delivery | Qualified options; existing shipping policy lists noon cutoff | Confirm serviced addresses, payment/stock cutoff, exceptions and available capacity |

| Price drops / best price | Current offers only; no nationwide lowest-price claim | Identical product/size/pack comparator, timestamp and genuine prior-price history |
| Seller spotlight | Work queue only | Seller permission, real identity/images, operational story and stock links |
| Customer unboxing content | Existing photo-review flow and delivered-order invitation | Actual customer material and publication permission; moderate PII and inappropriate uploads |
