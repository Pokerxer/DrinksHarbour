# Twelve-week content queue

Week 1 begins on the actual release date, which is not set. New commercial
guides are delivered in code; the work below is subsequent editorial operation.
Use a maintained /deals destination for current offers rather than making an
indexable thin duplicate for every weekly roundup.

Every week: review live available offers, compare the same bottle/volume/pack,
verify discount claims against the current purchase price, remove expired links,
and record who checked them and when. Publish a roundup only when there are
useful, verified offers. No fabricated price-drop history or evergreen countdown.

## Editorial acceptance

- A real responsible author/reviewer, sources for beverage-specific claims, and
  genuine publication/update dates; no invented expertise or testimonials.
- Unique practical value beyond a list of keywords. Link to the relevant
  canonical category, current product and delivery/policy page naturally.
- A selected bottle's origin, ABV and size must match the catalogue/offer.
  Tasting notes, awards, importer relationships and certification need evidence.
- Do not publish a fixed event package, price or delivery guarantee until the
  commercial team can fulfil it. Enquiry pages currently promise a conversation.
- Review invitations go to all eligible delivered customers without screening
  for positive sentiment. Existing review moderation/eligibility stays enforced.
- Current review/photo support and the new delivered-order invitation do not
  authorise automated email/WhatsApp requests or publishing someone's photos.
- Do not create another city page unless actual service details and demand
  justify it. City-name substitutions are not sufficient editorial content.
