## Placement verification record

When a link appears, record its full source URL, destination URL, anchor text,
`rel` attributes, source-page response, canonical and robots directives, date
first observed, whether it existed before outreach, and editorial/sponsored status.
Confirm the link is present and clickable on the actual published page.
Keep indexing unknown unless independently checked; do not infer it from HTTP 200.
