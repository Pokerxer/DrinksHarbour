## Recommended sequence

1. Confirm the sender's real name, role and sending account. Verify the pairing
   guide's attribution, source claims and current product availability before pitching.
2. Start with Drinkabl Media, then AbujaEats. Check whether Visit Abuja accepts
   online services as resources; do not request a restaurant listing for the platform.
3. Use The Drink Spectator and about-drinks only with an editorial angle their
   audience would find useful. Recheck contact forms before sending.
4. Pursue stockist/customer mentions only through confirmed trading relationships.
   Restaurant and supplier prospects are not asserted to be existing partners.
5. After explicit user authorization to send, send individual pitches and record
   dates and replies. Follow up once after seven days; close after another seven
   days without a response. These are suggested intervals, not scheduled actions.

## Quality rules

Let editors choose whether and how to cite a useful source. Use the brand or
article title naturally; do not demand exact-match anchors or follow links.
Do not exchange products, money or reciprocal links for ranking credit.
Paid placements require disclosure and appropriate link qualification, such as
`rel="sponsored"`; treat them as advertising with a separate budget decision.

Google's [link spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam)
supports these distinctions. Do not disavow based on a provider spam score alone.

## Measurement

Measure verified new relevant referring domains, actual linking pages, referral
visits and conversions when analytics is available, and non-brand Search Console
clicks to the cited pages. Record link attributes and whether the source page is
accessible. A published link and an indexable source page do not prove indexing
or ranking impact. No placement, traffic, authority or ranking gains are promised.
