## Approach

Start with Everyday, Ambick and Kosman after confirming current operations and the person
responsible for stock/sales. Ask one workflow question before presenting a demo. Do not
claim they have stock losses, lack a POS, need a new website or want to replace software.
Keep any first message short and grounded in the specific published fact above.

Drinks.ng and WineShopAbuja were also reviewed but not included in this initial import:
their established commerce offerings require stronger integration/partnership qualification.
Benfantic was excluded because its retrieved page mixed a Port Harcourt delivery claim
with an Anambra address and an unreplaced store-address placeholder. This is a source-quality
concern, not evidence that the business is illegitimate.

Before contacting any record, check it is not an existing tenant, identify the appropriate
business decision-maker, recheck the public contact, and obtain message approval.
Reoon validation establishes mailbox properties, not permission or interest.
