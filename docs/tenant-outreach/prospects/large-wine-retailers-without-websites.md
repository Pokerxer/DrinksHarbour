# Established wine retailers without websites

Current additional gate: the store must primarily sell beverages. General
supermarkets are excluded even when they sell wine. Read [target-profile.md](target-profile.md).

User direction supersedes the broad prospect batches: prioritise Nigerian
businesses selling wine to retail customers, with evidence of operating scale,
and no current business-owned website. Do not delete the earlier research.

## Qualification rules

1. Wine retail must be evidenced; a beer distributor, importer or bar is not
   automatically a wine retailer.
2. Scale needs independent evidence: multiple active stores, substantial published
   operating capacity, or identifiable retail/distribution operations. Names such
   as Megastore, stockist status and social follower counts alone do not prove size.
3. Search the business name, aliases, parent entity, social links and historical
   domains. A Carrd page or another hosted business site still counts as a website.
4. Missing website fields and failed fetches mean website status is unconfirmed.
   Only a current business confirmation can establish that it has no website.
5. Keep pending candidates separate from fully qualified matches. No candidate in
   this batch meets all three requirements with high confidence yet.
