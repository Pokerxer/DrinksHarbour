## Qualification question

For every candidate: How many beverage retail stores are active, are wines and
other drinks the main merchandise, and what business-owned website or online
ordering page do you use today? Then ask how stock, shop sales and customer
orders are currently recorded. Do not presume a software gap or promise ROI.
