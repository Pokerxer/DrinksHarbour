# Current target

This supersedes earlier broad supermarket/distributor prospect batches.

All gates must be checked before a business is promoted:

1. **Beverage specialist:** wines, spirits, champagne, beer, mixers or soft drinks
   form the core retail offer. Merely stocking wine is insufficient. Confirm the
   store's merchandise focus; do not invent sales percentages.
2. **Retail:** sells bottles/cases to end customers. Wholesale plus retail is
   acceptable; wholesale-only businesses and manufacturers are outside scope.
3. **Established scale:** multiple active stores or substantiated substantial
   beverage retail operations. Names, directory categories and follower counts
   alone do not establish size.
4. **Website filter:** existing business websites are allowed, explicitly
   authorized by the user on 23 October 2026. No-site requirements in older
   batches are historical. Do not infer a software gap from website presence.


Exclude general supermarkets, pharmacies, mixed household/FMCG stores, bars,
lounges and restaurants as the primary prospect. Retain old evidence as history.
The user may expand this scope later; do not do so implicitly.
