Two things worth saying plainly before anything else.

**First, where we are.** Drinks Harbour is live and trading — the site is up, the catalogue is stocked and we are fulfilling orders in Abuja. We are early, though, and order volume is still building. I would rather tell you that now than quote you an audience figure I cannot stand behind. What we can offer is early ownership of a category while it is still available, and content assets that keep working long after any single campaign ends.

**Second, listing costs you nothing.** Stocking and selling your products on our platform carries no listing fee, no slotting fee and no shelf charge. Visibility — homepage placement, category banners, editorial — is the part that is paid, and it is entirely optional. I have set it out separately below so the two decisions stay separate.

## Onboarding — what we need and how it runs

Our catalogue is a single central product database, so each SKU is created once and carries full product data. To open a brand file for Jambitts we need:

**Company and compliance**
- CAC certificate and TIN
- NAFDAC registration numbers for each SKU
- Distributor authorisation letter for Bold '98, Santa Alba and Patagon
- Bank details for settlement

**Commercial**
- Full catalogue and trade price list, with recommended retail prices
- Minimum order quantity and case configuration per SKU
- Lead time from order to delivery in Abuja, and delivery terms
- Payment terms, and whether you can support sale-or-return on any lines
- Any volume rebates or promotional allowances

**Product data — per SKU** (this is what the platform stores, and what makes a page rank)
- Bottle size in ml, ABV, country and region of origin
- Tasting and flavour notes, cask or grape detail, vintage where applicable
- EAN/barcode, case size, shelf life
- Awards or medals, if any
- Product images: transparent-background or clean white-background bottle shots, 2000px minimum on the longest side

**Samples.** Yes please — one bottle per SKU. We use them for our own photography, tasting notes and staff training. We will confirm a delivery address once we speak.

**Process and timing.** Once documents and the price list are in, we load your SKUs, write the product copy and shoot the photography, and the range goes live on the site. Realistically that is two to three weeks from receiving everything — sooner on the first few SKUs if you want a lead line up before the full range.

## The commercial route

Two options, and I would like your view on which suits Jambitts better:

1. **Wholesale.** We buy at your trade price and carry the stock. Simplest for you, and it is the route we would prefer for the fastest-moving SKUs.
2. **Consignment.** We hold the stock, you retain title, and we settle on sale at an agreed margin. Lower risk for us at this stage, which means we can carry more of your range from the start rather than testing one or two SKUs.

A mixed arrangement — wholesale on the core lines, consignment on the deeper range — is usually where this lands, and we are open to it.

## Promotional support

You mentioned promotional support to drive sales, which is the right instinct — a listing on a new platform without visibility behind it will not do much for either of us. I have attached our rate card and a seven-piece content plan built specifically around your three brands.

Briefly, it covers homepage hero placement, category banners in Whiskey and Wine, commissioned articles, social sets, newsletter features and product photography, with three packages at founding rates held for twelve months. The content plan includes pieces such as *Irish Whiskey vs Scotch: Which One Should You Actually Start With?* and *Wine with Jollof, Suya and Pepper Soup* — search phrases nobody in this market currently owns, which is precisely why publishing now is worth more than publishing later. Growing our order volume is the work in front of us, and this is a large part of how we intend to do it.

The rate card also carries a make-good clause: if the site does not reach an agreed monthly sessions floor during a paid placement window, we extend that placement month for month at no cost until it does. Given our stage, that seemed the only fair way to price it.

None of this is a condition of listing. If you would rather simply have the brands on the platform and revisit promotion once you have seen how they sell, that is a perfectly good answer.
