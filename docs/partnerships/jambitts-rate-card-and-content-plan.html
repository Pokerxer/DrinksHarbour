Where we are, plainly

Drinks Harbour is live and trading — the site is up, the catalogue is stocked and orders are being fulfilled in Abuja. We are early, though, and order volume is still building. Our numbers today are those of a young business, not an established one.

We would rather state that up front than sell you an impressions figure we cannot stand behind. What is on offer here is early ownership of a category while it is still available, and permanent content assets that accumulate search value from the day they publish — independent of what our traffic looks like this month.

Listing is free. Visibility is paid. Stocking and selling your products costs you nothing. This document prices only the promotional placement and editorial work that sits on top of it.

The content plan

Seven pieces, written by us, published in this order. Each one targets a search phrase nobody in this market currently owns — which is the whole point of publishing now rather than later.

01
Irish Whiskey vs Scotch: Which One Should You Actually Start With?

Most Nigerian drinkers who avoid whiskey avoid it because Scotch bit them first. Irish whiskey's smoothness is the honest answer to that, and Bold '98 is the bottle the piece lands on.

Brand Bold '98 · Intent Category, top of funnel
02
Wine with Jollof, Suya and Pepper Soup: A Pairing Guide That Works

Every pairing guide online assumes European food. This is the piece we expect to travel furthest — it is genuinely useful, highly shareable, and no one is competing for it. Santa Alba and Patagon carry the recommendations.

Brand Santa Alba, Patagon · Intent Shareable, link-earning
03
Bold '98 Irish Whiskey: Tasting Notes, Price and Where to Buy in Abuja

Claims the brand's own name in search before anyone else does. Anyone who sees a bottle, a bar list or an advert and looks it up should land on us.

Brand Bold '98 · Intent Brand + transactional
04
Why Chilean Wine Is the Best Value on the Shelf Right Now

Positions the whole Santa Alba range against French and Italian bottles at twice the price — the argument that actually converts a wine buyer in this market.

Brand Santa Alba · Intent Category, top of funnel
05
Five Irish Whiskey Cocktails You Can Make at Home Tonight

Irish coffee, whiskey sour, hot toddy, ginger highball and a house serve built on Bold '98. Long-tail traffic with a seasonal lift every December.

Brand Bold '98 · Intent Long tail, recurring
06
Santa Alba and Patagon: Which Bottle for Which Occasion

A range guide that does the choosing for the customer. Every bottle links straight to its product page, so the piece sells as well as it informs.

Brand Santa Alba, Patagon · Intent Transactional
07
What to Put on the Table: A Host's Guide to Nigerian Celebrations

Timed for wedding and December season, when spend concentrates and people buy by the case rather than the bottle. All three brands feature.

Brand All three · Intent Seasonal, high basket value

How it runs

Week 1
Agreement signed. Catalogue, price list, NAFDAC and company documents received. Samples delivered. SKUs loaded to the platform.
Week 2
Product photography shot from samples. Tasting notes written. First two articles drafted and sent to you for factual review.
Week 3
Homepage hero goes live. Articles 01 and 02 publish. First social set runs.
Week 4+
One article per fortnight thereafter. Monthly report to you: sessions, article performance, units sold.

Terms

  • Payment. 50% on signature, 50% on go-live. Founding rates held for 12 months.
  • Make-good. If the site does not reach [agreed sessions floor] monthly sessions during a paid placement window, we extend that placement month-for-month at no cost until it does.
  • Editorial control. We write the articles and retain editorial judgement. You get factual review — spelling of the brand, ABV, provenance, price — not copy approval. Content that reads like an advert does not rank and does not get trusted, which serves neither of us.
  • Disclosure. Commissioned pieces carry a brand-partnership note.
  • Assets. Your supplied images and marks remain yours. Photography we shoot from samples is licensed to both parties, unrestricted.
  • Compliance. All placements are age-gated to 18+, carry responsible-consumption messaging, and are never targeted to under-age audiences.
  • Listing is separate. Consignment or wholesale terms are agreed in a supply agreement and are not conditional on any spend in this document.
