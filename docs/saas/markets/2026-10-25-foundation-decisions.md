## Engineering decisions

- Preserve separate admin/platform/API/mobile applications and the external Wyn website. Shared services and country configuration provide one product; no physical app merger.
- Preserve Tenant/Product/SubProduct/Size/Warehouse identities and current plan entitlements. Separate marketplace/storefront enrollment from ERM subscription; marketplace rejection does not cancel ERM.
- Nigeria/NGN remains the existing path with the new server feature disabled. New country enforcement requires explicit saved active reviewed country, dated policy, enrollment and channel terms. No automatic production seed/backfill or startup index migration.
- Country is a trading context, not product origin or incorporation country. Public market preference is never tenant or account authority. Direct website context comes from its saved issuing-key/channel mapping.
- Store immutable country/channel/payment-owner and pricing-term snapshots for new opt-in commitments. Existing receipt recovery and accepted fulfillment do not depend on current publication approval. Historical orders remain legacy where evidence is absent.
- Keep current revenue agreement and direct retail pricing. A terms-reference field does not invent or calculate new fees. Different gateway/account ownership cannot be selected by the browser.
- Country language/currency configuration advertises supported transaction settings only; it does not claim translated content or FX conversion. International price/tax/address/reporting work remains its own package.
