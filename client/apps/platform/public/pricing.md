# Pricing — DrinksHarbour for beverage businesses

DrinksHarbour is an online beverage marketplace based in Abuja, Nigeria
(https://www.drinksharbour.com). Shoppers buy drinks on the marketplace;
beverage businesses (shops, bars, distributors, venues) pay a monthly
subscription for a branded storefront and inventory/order management tools.

This file describes the subscription plans for **beverage businesses**.
Customer-facing drink prices are in Nigerian Naira (NGN, ₦) and shown on live
product pages; they are not listed here.

Last updated: 2026-11-10. Prices are billed monthly in NGN via Paystack.
Source of truth: `server/config/erm-plans.js` in the DrinksHarbour repository.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Product listings (SKUs) | Staff accounts | Marketplace commission |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | ₦15,000/month | Up to 100 | 1 | 13% |
| Growth | ₦35,000/month | Up to 500 | 3 | 11% |
| Pro | ₦65,000/month | Up to 2,000 | 10 | 10% |
| Enterprise | ₦85,000/month | Unlimited | Unlimited | 9% |
| Venue | ₦150,000/month | Unlimited | Unlimited | 9% |

Commission is the percentage DrinksHarbour takes on marketplace sales made for
the business; the business keeps the rest.

## What each plan includes

- **All plans:** branded storefront on drinksharbour.com, inventory management,
  order processing, and sales invoicing.
- **Starter:** point-of-sale (POS) at a single outlet, basic CRM, purchase
  orders for restocking.
- **Growth:** everything in Starter, plus multi-outlet POS.
- **Pro:** everything in Growth, plus advanced CRM, multi-location support,
  advanced reports, and API access.
- **Enterprise:** everything in Pro, plus custom integrations and priority
  support; no listing or staff limits.
- **Venue:** everything in Pro, plus real-time multi-outlet POS, table
  management, guest CRM, event booking, and real-time bar inventory, for
  bars, clubs and lounges.

## Free trial

New businesses can start with a 14-day free trial (up to 50 listings, 1 staff
account). Registration starts at https://www.drinksharbour.com/vendors/register.

## Multi-location add-ons (Pro and above)

- **Extra shop:** ₦12,000/month
- **Extra warehouse:** ₦20,000/month

The first extra shop and the first extra warehouse are included free with a
plan; these prices are per additional unit, billed monthly as their own
subscriptions.

## Custom / enterprise scale

Businesses needing negotiated terms or higher volumes can request a custom
contract through https://www.drinksharbour.com/contact .
