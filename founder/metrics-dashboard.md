## Ignore these as success measures

- **Total signups:** easy to inflate; follow collected subscription value and renewal.
- **Gross marketplace GMV:** large tenant proceeds can hide platform losses; follow actual net contribution and cohort recovery.
- **Number of modules:** delivery cost can increase with breadth; follow retained payment and support economics.
- **Annual cash collected as monthly profit:** future service remains owed; separate service-period revenue from cash runway.

## Minimal tracking setup

No new analytics subscription or application code is needed for the first review. Maintain local CSV ledgers with references to existing invoices, settlement exports and support records. Protect tenant/customer identifiers; avoid raw card data and unnecessary personal information.

| Need | Tool | Incremental tool cost | Estimated setup |
|---|---|---:|---:|

No tool installed. Use an existing spreadsheet app if already available. Do not add cloud tracking until useful.

## Twelve events to record

Manual ledger entries are sufficient initially. Event date, tenant/cohort, country/currency, amount or duration, evidence reference and channel must accompany applicable events.

| Event | When recorded | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| demo_completed | Qualified decision-maker sees relevant full flow | Sales funnel denominator |
| pilot_agreed | Scope, price and dates accepted | Commitment, not collected revenue |
| subscription_paid | Verified invoice payment | Collection and service period |
| setup_completed | Agreed onboarding accepted | Setup cost and earned fee |
| tenant_activated | Operator completes receipt, sale and stock reconciliation with correct balances | Operational use |
| support_logged | Support work occurs, with minutes | Real cost-to-serve |
| renewal_due | Contract period ends | Retention denominator |
| renewal_paid | Renewal payment verifies | Paid retention |
| cancellation_recorded | Tenant cancels or eligible renewal fails | Churn reason and amount |
| marketplace_order_fulfilled | Merchant confirms delivery/collection | Completed GMV and attribution |
| order_financially_reconciled | Settlement, provider charges and costs checked | Contribution evidence |
| refund_or_loss_recorded | Refund, reversal or loss occurs | Cohort economics correction |

These are proposed evidence records, not implemented telemetry or proof of successful payment handling.
